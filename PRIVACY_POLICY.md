# FocusBoard — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 27, 2026**

FocusBoard ("FocusBoard", "we", "our", or "the app") is a productivity application designed to help users organize their goals, missions, tasks, notes, and focus sessions.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and what choices you have when using FocusBoard.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

FocusBoard is designed to minimize the amount of personal information required to use the application.

Depending on the features you use, the application may process the following categories of information:

### 1.1 Information You Create

You may voluntarily create and store information such as:

- tasks;
- missions;
- projects;
- goals;
- notes;
- focus sessions;
- tags and categories;
- reminders;
- productivity preferences;
- application settings.

This information is used to provide the application's functionality.

### 1.2 Device and Technical Information

Certain technical information may be processed automatically by third-party services used by the application.

This may include:

- device type;
- operating system;
- application version;
- approximate technical information;
- advertising identifiers;
- diagnostic information;
- crash or performance information.

The exact information collected may depend on the services enabled on your device.

---

## 2. Local Storage

FocusBoard uses local storage technologies, including Android's local database capabilities, to store application data on your device.

This may include:

- tasks;
- missions;
- goals;
- notes;
- focus sessions;
- preferences;
- other application data necessary for the application's operation.

Local data may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the application's data, uninstall the application, or use an available deletion feature.

---

## 3. Advertising

FocusBoard Free may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect or process information such as:

- advertising identifiers;
- device information;
- IP address;
- approximate location information;
- interaction with advertisements;
- diagnostic and performance information.

This information may be used to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising, depending on the user's settings and applicable requirements.

FocusBoard Premium is designed to provide an ad-free experience.

We do not sell your personal information directly to advertisers.

---

## 4. Google Play Billing

FocusBoard may offer optional Premium subscriptions through Google Play Billing.

Payments are processed by Google Play.

FocusBoard does not directly receive or store your complete payment card information.

Subscription information may be processed to determine whether your Premium entitlement is active.

This may include:

- subscription status;
- purchase status;
- product identifier;
- renewal or expiration information.

Payment processing is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

---

## 5. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, FocusBoard may send notifications related to features such as:

- task reminders;
- focus sessions;
- deadlines;
- recurring tasks;
- achievements;
- other productivity reminders.

You can disable notifications through Android system settings or available application settings.

---

## 6. Internet Access

FocusBoard may require Internet access for certain features, including:

- advertising;
- subscription verification;
- future synchronization;
- online services;
- application updates or remote configuration.

Core features designed for offline use may continue to function without an Internet connection.

---

## 7. Focus DNA

FocusBoard may provide a feature called **Focus DNA**, designed to help users understand their own work habits.

Depending on the implemented version, Focus DNA may analyze information such as:

- focus session duration;
- completed sessions;
- interruptions;
- task completion patterns;
- preferred focus periods;
- estimated versus actual duration.

Whenever technically possible, this analysis is performed locally on the device.

Focus DNA is intended to provide productivity insights and is not a medical, psychological, or diagnostic service.

---

## 8. Focus AI

Future versions of FocusBoard may provide optional AI-powered functionality.

If AI functionality requires information to be transmitted to an external AI service, the application will provide appropriate information about that processing before or when the feature is used.

Users should avoid submitting highly sensitive or confidential information to AI features unless explicitly informed otherwise.

---

## 9. Data Sharing

FocusBoard does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to operate specific application features.

These services may include:

- Google AdMob for advertising;
- Google Play services for application distribution and subscription functionality;
- other infrastructure or service providers introduced in future versions.

Third-party services process information according to their own applicable policies and terms.

---

## 10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by FocusBoard.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should keep their Android device protected with an appropriate screen lock and security settings.

---

## 11. Data Retention

Local application data generally remains on your device until you:

- delete it through the application, where supported;
- clear the application's storage;
- uninstall FocusBoard;
- or otherwise remove the data from your device.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to those services' respective policies and retention practices.

---

## 12. Children's Privacy

FocusBoard is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating an account or using the application.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can evaluate the situation and take appropriate action.

Depending on the features available in your version of FocusBoard, you may be able to:

- delete locally stored application data;
- disable notifications;
- control advertising preferences through Android or Google settings;
- cancel a Premium subscription through Google Play;
- uninstall the application.

You may also contact us regarding privacy-related questions.
